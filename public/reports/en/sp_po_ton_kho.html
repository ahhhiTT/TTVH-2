📘 User guide: read before running

Goal: The tool turns Sales M8 + Sales M9 MTD + current Inventory into a list of SKUs to restock, with priority, OOS risk and the reason for each recommendation.

  1. Prepare 3 Excel files: Sales M8 (full month), Sales M9 MTD (up to today) and an Inventory Snapshot.
  2. Upload the right file: box ① = M8 full month, box ② = M9 MTD (e.g. 01–15/09), box ③ = current stock on the calculation date.
  3. Check the assumptions: Calculation date is the stock cut-off date; Lead time is the number of days from ordering to arrival; Growth M9/M10 is the assumed growth; Revenue coverage is the share of revenue to focus on; Safety stock is the buffer stock.
  4. Click Generate V2.1. To see how the tool works without your data, click Load demo.
  5. Read Dashboard → Today's Action first to see which SKUs need action now.
  6. Open PO Recommendation to see PO, coverage, OOS, emergency gap and buy timing.
  7. Open Core 80% to check the SKU group that makes up most of the revenue.
  8. Open OOS Risk to prioritise SKUs at risk of stock-out before ETA.
  9. Open SKU Diagnosis to understand why the tool made each recommendation.
  10. Use Export recommendation CSV to export the SKUs with PO > 0.
📂 Which columns must the Excel files have?

Sales: The tool looks for the columns Mã SKU, Số lượng hàng bán, Doanh số hiệu quả and Tên hàng hoá. If there is a date column (Ngày / Ngày tạo đơn / Order Date), the tool can work out the number of MTD days.

Inventory: The tool needs Mã SKU and Tồn kho sẵn sàng bán. Rows with the same SKU are summed.

File names do not have to match. The tool tries to find the header row and recognise equivalent columns.

Note: If the file has several warehouses, V2.1 sums stock per SKU. Only upload warehouse data that matches the planning scope.

⚙️ What the parameters mean

Calculation date: the stock snapshot date used for the calculation. For 15/09, the remaining September demand is counted from 16/09. Sales M9 is read as MTD up to this date.

Lead time: number of days from placing the PO until the goods can arrive at the warehouse.

Current month growth: baseline fallback when there are no MTD sales yet or MTD has too little signal. With M9 MTD, the forecast blends the M8 baseline with the M9 run-rate, weighting M9 more as data days increase.

Next month growth: growth used to forecast the next month.

Revenue coverage: e.g. 80% means the tool picks SKUs in revenue order until about 80% of revenue is covered; the SKU that crosses the threshold is kept.

Safety stock: buffer stock added to planning demand. V2.1 defaults to 10%, adjustable.

🧠 How does the tool classify SKUs?

V2.1 uses 3 separate views so that “growth” is not confused with “importance”:

1. Business importance: Core or Long-tail based on revenue contribution.

2. Demand momentum: High Growth / Stable / Declining / New based on sales and growth.

3. Inventory risk: Critical / At Risk / Healthy / Excess based on coverage and lead time.

So a SKU with only +22% growth can still be Core + Critical if its revenue contribution is large and its stock is low.

🚨 How to read the PO Recommendation

Coverage: how many days current stock lasts at the forecast daily demand.

Estimated OOS: expected stock-out date if demand follows the forecast.

Emergency Gap: shortfall needed to cover demand during the lead time. If > 0, the SKU risks a stock-out before the PO arrives.

Recommended PO: quantity to add to cover demand over the planning horizon plus safety stock, after current stock.

Buy Timing: ORDER NOW = stock-out risk before ETA; ORDER SOON = low coverage close to lead time; RECHECK = no need to buy yet.

Forecast Confidence: 1–2 MTD days = Low; 3–5 = Medium-Low; 6–10 = Medium; 11 days or more = High. M9 weights are 20%, 30%, 50%, 70%, 85% respectively.

Priority: P0 Critical → P1 High → P2 Medium → P4 No Buy.

⚠️ 5 things to check before using the results for a real PO
  1. Does the stock snapshot date match the Calculation date?
  2. Sales uses the right metric Số lượng hàng bán, not gift items or order count.
  3. Do SKUs in Sales and Inventory use the same codes and the same warehouse scope?
  4. Does the growth assumption fit the upcoming business plan/promotions?
  5. Check the PO recommendation against promotions, supplier constraints, MOQ and incoming POs before placing a real order. These are not yet full inputs of V2.1.
